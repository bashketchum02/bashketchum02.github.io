In a dilemma. life is mostly okay, its nothing grand but I am living a pretty okay life. But it is jarring, because part of my brain thinks I have everything I need, while part of me craves adventure, and see what's out there.

I see many friends doing many things, and having adventures. Sometimes I feel like my job, my location and everything else if stopping me from having the same experiences. But I think that is false, I don't even go out on adventures where I live (Malaysia). I rarely travel, rarely engage in local communities, and honestly rarely do anything at all. In my 8 years in Malaysia, I have never once went to see a different city and have a change of air, meet the locals outside my bubble etc.

I have a version of my myself in my mind that thinks - "Oh if I moved across the world it will be different", but I doubt so. I doubt if I am not have adventures now, I will have an adventure then.

So maybe, the best idea is - right here and right now, have an adventure, and experience the community, the people, the places.

As a start I have two things in mind:

1. Visit Kundasang (my first solo travel!)

2. Engage with people while I am travelling.

I will try to write more about it as I go on.
